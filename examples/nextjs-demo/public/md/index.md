# Aurora Fieldwork

Aurora Fieldwork is a two-person consultancy that plans and runs cold-weather environmental surveys. We were founded in Tromso in 2019 and we have completed 63 field deployments across Norway, Iceland, and Greenland since then. Most of our work is glacier monitoring, permafrost borehole instrumentation, and the slow, unglamorous business of keeping sensors alive through a polar winter.

## What we do

- Survey design and permitting for sites above 66 degrees north.
- Sensor installation, calibration, and seasonal retrieval.
- Data cleaning and delivery in open formats, never a proprietary bundle.

## Recent work

In the 2024 season we maintained 18 automated weather stations for three university partners and lost exactly one to a storm, which we consider a good year. Our published uptime across the fleet was 97.4 percent.
