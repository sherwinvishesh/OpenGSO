# About

Aurora Fieldwork is Ingrid Halle and Petter Roald. Ingrid spent nine years at the Norwegian Polar Institute before leaving to do fieldwork full time. Petter is an instrumentation engineer who has built loggers for high-latitude deployments since 2011.

## How we work

1. A scoping call, always free, usually about forty minutes.
2. A written survey plan with a fixed price and a named weather contingency window.
3. Deployment, then a retrieval trip in the following season.
4. Raw data plus a cleaned dataset, delivered as CSV and NetCDF.

## What we will not do

> We do not take work that requires flying a helicopter into a closed weather window. There is no deadline worth that, and we have turned down three contracts over it.

## Where we are

Our workshop is in Tromso, Norway. Most of the year one of us is somewhere else, so email is far better than a phone call.
